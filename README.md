# Awesome Opsgenie Alternatives [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of alternatives to Opsgenie for on-call scheduling, alerting, and incident management — grouped by the kind of team they fit best.

Teams look for Opsgenie alternatives for all kinds of reasons: pricing at scale, on-call UX, integration depth, or just wanting a second opinion. This list doesn't rank them — it groups them so you can start from the category that matches your team.

## Enterprise-Grade

- [PagerDuty](https://www.pagerduty.com) - Enterprise incident management and on-call platform.
- [VictorOps (Splunk On-Call)](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/products/on-call.html) - Incident response and on-call, part of Splunk.
- [xMatters](https://www.xmatters.com) - IT service and incident management platform.

## SRE / Developer-Focused

- [FireHydrant](https://firehydrant.com) - Incident management and reliability platform.
- [incident.io](https://incident.io) - Incident management built around Slack.
- [Rootly](https://rootly.com) - Incident management platform integrated with Slack.
- [Spike](https://spike.sh) - Incident management and on-call platform with real-time alerting.
- [Squadcast](https://www.squadcast.com) - SRE-focused incident response platform.

## Budget-Friendly & Startup-Focused

- [AlertOps](https://www.alertops.com) - Incident alerting and on-call platform.
- [ilert](https://www.ilert.com) - Incident management, on-call, and status pages.
- [Zenduty](https://www.zenduty.com) - Incident management and on-call platform for SRE teams.

## Open Source & Self-Hosted

- [Cabot](https://github.com/arachnys/cabot) - Self-hosted on-call and monitoring alerting, PagerDuty-style, open source.
- [GoAlert](https://goalert.me) - Open-source on-call scheduling and alerting, built by Target ([source](https://github.com/target/goalert)).
- [Iris](https://github.com/linkedin/iris) - LinkedIn's open-source highly configurable alerting and on-call system.
